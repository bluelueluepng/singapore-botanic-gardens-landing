Miss Joaquim
Papilionanthe Miss Joaquim
Singapore’s national flower, and the only hybrid in the world chosen as one. Bred by Agnes Joaquim and first recorded by the Gardens’ director H. N. Ridley in 1893, it was chosen for its resilience and year-round blooms.
FAMILY Orchidaceae
PARENTS P. teres × P. hookeriana
FIRST RECORDED 24 June 1893
